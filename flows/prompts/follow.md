Without asking anyone: if you wrote a change, commit everything you wrote on this branch as one commit. Its subject proposes the change in this repository's commit style (see git log {target}); its body says what the proposal asks for and why, for someone reading the history later, and ends with the line '{refs}'. Then write to {run}/summary, for the person who will review it, the change's open questions, the consequential ones (behavior, scope, compatibility, data, costly interfaces) first, each beginning with the marker [consequential], the routine ones after, unmarked. If you wrote no document, commit nothing and write to {run}/declined, for the person who filed {ref}, a few plain lines on why no proposal could be drafted and what the request would need.
